## Notes
Kenney tiles are used unmodified from the packs (files under `town/`).
`chars/` spritesheets are extracted cells from DawnLike's Player sheets
(2 walk frames × 4 directions per character). `props/treeA.png` and
`props/treeB.png` are compositions of four Kenney canopy tiles plus trunk
tiles. Door-state lights, nameplates, and UI elements are original KNOCK code.
