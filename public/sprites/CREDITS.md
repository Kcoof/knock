## Notes
Kenney tiles under `town/` are unmodified; `town2x/` are the same tiles
nearest-neighbor upscaled 2x for the 32px world. `chars/` spritesheets are
extracted DawnLike Player cells (2 walk frames × 4 directions per character).
`props/treeA.png`, `props/treeB.png` and `props/bench.png` are original KNOCK
pixel art. Door-state lights, nameplates, terrain auto-painting, and UI are
original KNOCK code.
